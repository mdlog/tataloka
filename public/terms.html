The service

Tataloka generates interior-design suggestions from a room photo or a room type using AI, shows them in 3D and on a floor plan, and links to products sold by third-party stores. The free plan is provided free of charge, as is, and may change or be unavailable at any time.

Credits and plans

  • AI features use credits. Every account gets a free monthly allowance (currently 30 credits) that refills on the 1st of each month, Jakarta time (WIB). Unused free credits do not carry over.
  • Credits are used only when an AI call succeeds; credits for failed calls are returned automatically. Editing, saving and exporting designs do not use credits.
  • Credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred, sold or exchanged for money.
  • Paid plans and credit packs are not on sale yet. If they are introduced, their price and terms will be shown before you pay.

AI results and prices

  • Designs, room measurements, renders and product choices are generated by AI and may be inaccurate. Check measurements before buying.
