Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Tataloka (“we”) is an AI interior-design app at tataloka.com. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account data — your name and email address. If you sign in with Google we also receive your Google account ID and profile picture (scopes: openid email profile). If you register with email, we store only a salted scrypt hash of your password, never the password itself.
  • Saved designs — the designs you create while logged in: room type, style, room size, the products in the design and their placement, prices and budget.
  • AI renders — when you generate an AI render while logged in, the resulting image is saved with that design so you can open it again from My designs. If you uploaded a photo, the render shows your room restyled. It is replaced when you render again, removed when you change the design’s style, and deleted when you delete the design. Only you can view it.
  • Credits — your plan, credit balance and a history of credit use (which AI feature, how many credits, when). It does not include the photos or text you sent.
  • Session cookie — one HTTP-only cookie (tl_session) that keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. Preferences such as language and budget are kept in your browser’s local storage.

What we do not store

  • Room photos you upload are used only to generate your design and are not stored on our server (only the AI render made from them, as described above).
  • Room videos never leave your device: your browser picks a few still frames from the video, and only those frames are sent to generate the design — like photos, they are not stored.
  • Voice recordings are used only to transcribe your command and are not stored.

How your data is used

Only to provide the app: logging you in, generating and saving your designs, and protecting the service from abuse (rate limits). We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Services we rely on

  • Meta Model API (SAM 3.1, Muse Spark, Muse Image, Muse Voice) processes your photo, design requests and voice commands to produce results.
  • Google provides “Sign in with Google” when you choose it.
  • Cloudflare delivers the website securely.
  • Product links open IKEA Indonesia, Tokopedia or Shopee; their own privacy policies apply on their sites.

Your choices

  • Delete any saved design at any time from My designs.
  • Log out at any time; this ends your session immediately.
  • To delete your account and all its data, email info@tataloka.com from the address on your account.

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, session tokens are stored only as SHA-256 hashes, cookies are HTTP-only and Secure over HTTPS, and each account can only access its own designs.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@tataloka.com.
